## Goal and scope

Remove repeated tracing of completed same-view work, retain honest uncertainty and test whether filtering reduces the remaining quality cost. Existing adaptive sampling, worker progression and à-trous denoising are present; extend them. Own `progressiverender.py`, `progressivejob.py`, `pathtrace.py`, `gpupathtrace.py`, `ptdenoise.py` and renderer tests. No temporal radiance reuse across changed simulation frames and no learned-model downloads.

## Deliver, in order, with a commit per part

1. Persist raw radiance sums, sample count/index, luminance moments and active-pixel masks across same-estimator refinement calls, for CPU and GPU. `_Frame.accumulator` currently clears samples and `progressiverender` retraces doubling passes; preserve allocation reuse while introducing explicit continuation. Treat different resolution, bounce budget, scene/frame/material/light/camera or quality settings as different estimators. Never add the approximate preview's samples into the faithful full-resolution solution.
2. Maintain bounded pass slices and cancellation generations so stale work cannot publish or contaminate the new view. Reuse guides while their inputs are unchanged. Resume variance-based adaptation with minimum-sample guards, and distinguish converged, capped, cancelled and time-budget exits. Keep denoised images out of raw sums. Add deterministic split-pass versus uninterrupted equivalence tests, independent sample-index tests, reset tests for every identity input and persistent-buffer release/eviction tests.
3. First measure sample continuation with the current à-trous filter. If filtering quality still determines the settle time, make a separately committed, optional comparison using an already available OIDN/OptiX installation; do not fetch weights or make vendor-specific runtime mandatory. Reuse device/filter objects, measure initialization/guides/copies/filter separately, compare beauty-only and guide-assisted results on liquid/steam, preserve alpha and show raw data. On absent/unsupported devices use the existing filter or CPU route; record unavailable comparisons. Reject denoising that hides wisps, distorts refraction, ghosts on edits or appears converged only by smoothing.
