## Question and today's evidence

Can full-resolution learned multi-frame flow improve large-motion/occlusion handling while retaining the existing analytic-motion accuracy and reducing the cost of supplying the current named vector layers?

The local **2026-09-27 V1** record in `docs/PARITY_2D.md` reports **1080p, four pyramid levels, eight iterations, both directions: 4.34 s CPU / 1.34 s RTX 3080 Ti**, plus **<0.1 px mean endpoint error** on synthetic translation/rotation. These are historical comparison points, not a performance measurement of today's HEAD. Reproduce the current implementation before comparing. The **2025 MEMFOF paper v1, Tables 2 and 8** reports **472 ms / 2.09 GB at full HD on RTX 3090**, with no AMP and a different inference contract. Never divide these numbers to advertise a NodeBased speedup.

Candidate: `egorchistov/optical-flow-MEMFOF-Tartan-T-TSKH`, immutable model revision **6c6c9aa3ad64f93aee8efbc2f7a6e4535814ee96** (HF API checked **2026-10-13**). Code/model card BSD-3-Clause. Use the general checkpoint; no Sintel/Spring fine-tune on a test from that same distribution. Source: [model card](https://huggingface.co/egorchistov/optical-flow-MEMFOF-Tartan-T-TSKH), [implementation](https://github.com/msu-video-group/memfof), [paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.23151v1).

## Inputs and flow contract — 15 minutes maximum

- Use the actual translation/rotation/disocclusion fixtures from `tests/test_opticalflow.py` and midpoint/blur constructions in `tests/test_flow_node_accuracy.py`. Retain their original sizes and scoring regions for the historical accuracy gate. Generate additional deterministic **1920 × 1080, five-frame** textured sequences with **32- and 64-pixel/frame** horizontal motion, subpixel motion, and a moving opaque foreground over a independently moving background. Save generation parameters/seed, analytic fields, visibility masks and digests. Added cases/settings are proposed tests, not current NodeBased benchmark results. Score newly exposed pixels separately; exclude undefined correspondence from EPE.
- Add one short existing, locally authorized production-like plate only if already available with its provenance. Without ground-truth vectors it supplies a blinded visual comparison/warp-consistency check, not an EPE claim. Missing plate means **synthetic-only**, not a failed prerequisite for the bounded algorithm comparison. Do not download a benchmark dataset or a new plate within this step.
- Prepare model RGB in its documented **0–255** convention; record transfer function, normalization and padding. Use the same source imagery for A; preserve baseline luminance conversion. If padding is required, crop back and ensure vectors remain in source-pixel units. No resizing to get a faster full-HD number.
- Official MEMFOF output is `(backward, forward)` **from the middle frame** of a triplet. Baseline `flow_pair(A,B)` supplies **A→B and B→A**, with different origin frames. Therefore a fair adjacent-pair measurement needs two overlapping triplets, or sequential feature reuse with its full accounting: select A→B from the triplet centred at A and B→A from that centred at B. Do not mistake middle→previous for the inverse of middle→next. Validate directions using known signed translations before running metrics. Exclude sequence endpoints from scored comparisons; propose endpoint fallback/cut resets explicitly rather than silently duplicating frames.
- Compare equivalent forward/backward occlusion masks using the existing consistency convention. Predicted flow through an occlusion is a hypothesis, not observed correspondence. Reuse the same threshold/scoring masks for A and B.

## Measurement and decision — 40 minutes maximum

- Measure cold model startup separately. For each scored central pair, perform **one warm-up and three timed repetitions**, synchronize before/after GPU regions, and report compute, host/device transfer, conversion, consistency-mask calculation and total wall time separately. Count every required triplet/feature computation. Record median and worst timing; do not invent a p95 from three repeats. Run two fresh-process repetitions of the fixed inputs for output hashes; report bit identity or max/mean differences plainly.
- Accuracy: report valid-visible EPE by small/large displacement and boundary band, occlusion-mask precision/recall, and downstream midpoint/warp pixel error. Keep baseline synthetic **<0.1 px** and unchanged static identity assertions as **no-regression gates**. On the new large-motion cases, proposed success is **at least 20% lower mean visible EPE** than matched A on the aggregate, with **no individual case worsening by more than 5%**, and no worse occlusion precision or recall. If A is already essentially exact, use absolute error and report no demonstrated opportunity rather than dividing by zero.
- Speed: proposed success is **≤0.67 s per equivalent full-HD adjacent pair including both directions and transfer/consistency work**, **and at least 2× faster than the current matched GPU baseline**. The absolute target is derived as half the dated **1.34 s** baseline; it is an experiment acceptance threshold, not a prediction. Feature amortization may be reported separately but cannot hide the first-use/cold cost.
- Resource/repeatability: remain within the explicit device guardrail and finish the deterministic-repeat check. Any analytic regression, exceeded resource budget, unaccounted direction/transfer work or missing matched baseline prevents a promotion recommendation, even if one flow image looks better. A quality win without the speed gate is a **quality-only follow-up candidate**, never a pass of the combined spike.
- Exercise the same computed flow through offline versions of the existing midpoint and motion-blur comparisons. Product wiring, interactive responsiveness and actual shared-cache invalidation remain unverified because this brief adds no backend. Keep the current CPU/GPU solver as the production fallback regardless of the outcome.
