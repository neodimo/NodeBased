# Research radar

## Generative video, swept 10/12

**2026-10-12 · Lane 3, T1 · Reading guide: ranking, evidence table, then decisions.**
Documentation research only: no weights downloaded, no GPU runs. Source dates below are publication/release dates where stated; undated living pages are explicitly marked **accessed 2026-10-12**. Published results belong to their stated hardware and configuration. Unknown means the cited sources do not establish the number, not that the technique cannot work.

### Ranked next moves

1. **Wan2.1 VACE-1.3B: first experiment.** Exported depth plus a reference image, joint video denoising, an existing diffusers pipeline and Apache-2.0 weights make this the shortest integration experiment. Its exact controlled workload on our card remains unmeasured.
2. **Wan2.1-Fun V1.1: strongest adjacent control option.** Separate depth/reference and camera checkpoints exist. The small camera model is particularly relevant to our exported poses. Do not assume its camera and depth checkpoints can be combined without implementation work.
3. **LTX-Video: fast reference-video alternative.** Small quantized models have a published consumer-card result. Depth IC-LoRA belongs to a larger model; that small-model result does not prove depth-control fit.
4. **HunyuanCustom / FramePack: low-memory reference experiments.** Useful identity and temporal methods, with a different stack and more restrictive base-model terms. They do not directly consume our scene lighting or camera matrices.
5. **Watch camera-aware methods and LTX-2.x.** ReCamMaster, CameraCtrl II, GEN3C and newer Wan camera/motion variants offer useful mechanisms. Read their control contracts before spending a step on heavier checkpoints.

#### The camera-control line

- **CameraCtrl** (paper 2024-04-02 [K]): camera intrinsics/extrinsics become per-pixel Plücker rays; temporal attention learns viewpoint changes. Released on AnimateDiffV3, with an SVD branch, in a custom stack. **CameraCtrl II** (2025-03-13 [K]) progressively expands viewpoint coverage while preserving dynamics. Neither checked source establishes a 12 GB, near-480p time; II's release/weight licence is unverified here. CameraCtrl adapter card is MIT; underlying AnimateDiff/SVD licences still apply. Do not assume SDXL compatibility.
- **MotionCtrl** (2023-12-06 [K]): separates camera extrinsics from object trajectories. This matches our need to distinguish a dolly from a moving cube. Custom stack; repo Apache-2.0 plus underlying checkpoint terms. Near-size frame-count/speed/12 GB controlled fit unverified.
- **ReCamMaster** (paper 2025-03-14; code 2025-04-09 [R]): conditions on a source video and target camera sequence, preserving dynamic timing. Released Wan-1.3B/DiffSynth implementation uses **81 × 832 × 480** and asserts **81 frames**; not a drop-in short-clip test. Code MIT, adapter card and Wan base Apache-2.0. Peak and speed unknown.
- **GEN3C** (release 2025-06-06 [G]): a persistent depth-derived 3D cache is rendered from the requested cameras before diffusion fills gaps. Closest conceptual match to our known scene geometry, but reported full-offload peak **43 GB** excludes the cited configuration. Recommended **121 camera samples**; comparable seconds/frame unknown. Custom Cosmos stack; code Apache-2.0, weights NVIDIA Open Model License. Its linked **Lyra** follow-up distils into static/dynamic splats; a research direction, not measured here.
- **Practical successor to test later:** Fun V1.1's released **1.3B Control-Camera** [F] offers the small Wan path; newer Fun-A14B-Control-Camera and Wan-Move [W2] expand camera/point control at a higher documented cost. No method's paper metric establishes our centimetre-level camera acceptance.

### 2. What could beat the current pipeline

**Temporal coherence — joint video denoising, with geometric controls.** VACE's shared spatiotemporal representation can coordinate texture and newly visible regions across the clip instead of repeatedly resampling a 2D canvas [V,D]. Depth anchors silhouettes; a reference anchors appearance. That is a plausible way to reduce the current softening/edge ghosting while lowering **2.6 / 7.7**, but no cited benchmark measures our shot. The renderer itself scores **0.19 / 0.30** in stage 8, demonstrating that a lower flicker number alone can reward simply retaining the layout. Require the requested rainy-street change and recognisable objects as well. Cost: the VACE-controlled peak and 3080 Ti runtime are unknown; sequential offload trades host RAM/transfers for VRAM. Apache-2.0 is straightforward, but fixed seeds still require a byte-repeat test. Avoid TeaCache, adaptive fallback or a new attention kernel in the first comparison: each introduces another numerical configuration.

**Lighting consistency — preserve shading evidence, not only depth.** A reference-conditioned video model can keep one appearance through time, yet a consistently wrong key light will still fail the verifier. Depth does not encode light direction, environment colour or shadow placement. The strongest low-cost hypothesis is to condition on the renderer's beauty/shading through masked video editing, or generate albedo once and shade it with the exported lights (section 4). VACE supports the former, but that is a different input experiment from depth-only conditioning [V]. HunyuanCustom preserves subjects, not physical lighting [H]. Cost: a beauty guide may resist the requested restyle; albedo needs de-lighting and materials. Neither establishes light-intensity correctness, which the present verifier explicitly leaves unchecked. Compare its actual direction, shadow and colour-balance observations, marking unavailable checks as unavailable. Model memory/licence costs follow the chosen row; renderer-only subsequent frames avoid repeated diffusion and offer a much simpler determinism contract.

**Camera adherence and objects — camera rays or geometry-backed reprojection.** CameraCtrl encodes rays, MotionCtrl separates camera and object movement, and ReCamMaster conditions video on new camera trajectories [K,R]. GEN3C goes further: render a persistent 3D cache from the requested pose, then complete missing content [G]. Our exported intrinsics, metric geometry and motion remove much of their depth/pose-estimation uncertainty. Fun's small camera checkpoint is the practical learned-camera follow-up [F]. Cost: camera-coordinate conversion, relative-pose/scale normalization and non-rigid object motion still need validation; learned control is approximate, and GEN3C's measured memory is outside our budget. Preserve the verifier's **1 cm / 0.25° / 0.1°** position/rotation/FOV gates from the conditioning contract. Object tracking may also reject changed appearance or ambiguous edges; inspect those matches before attributing a failure to geometry. A camera-conditioned model must be measured from its generated pixels; copying input depth/IDs or declaring camera support cannot substitute for that evidence. Licence and stack changes remain those in the landscape.

### 3. One worker-sized spike

[**VACE depth + reference against stage 8**](../briefs/proposed/T1-vace-depth-reference.md) specifies one model, one fixed offload strategy, aspect-preserving depth/reference preparation, two fresh runs and a 90-minute hard stop. It compares against the exact benchmark, requires better flicker without sacrificing the requested edit or worsening verification, and falls back to the unchanged current provider. This brief is proposed, not authorized execution. In particular, VACE's edit mask and our reprojection-validity mask have different meanings; the experiment keeps them separate.

### 4. Outside the box: three small tests

**Renderer light probes as a shading constraint.** Render neutral diffuse and glossy probe spheres under the exported lights/HDRI, alongside the scene's diffuse-only shading. Fit a small, temporally shared colour/shading correction to the generated sequence, keeping object masks and silhouettes fixed; test first on the existing stage 8 output, with no new model. Compare light-direction, shadow and colour-balance errors plus flicker before/after. This is a proposed experiment, not an existing provider capability. A colour correction cannot move an incorrect shadow: if the direction/shadow checks remain wrong, stop and use the shading render as an explicit future model condition. Cost is a renderer-pass adapter and a constrained fit; avoid calling it physical relighting without that evidence.

**Geometry-guided test-time correction with an appearance anchor.** Freeze the generated frames and optimize a low-resolution residual field using our depth/ID-valid backward reprojection correspondences, penalizing temporal disagreement, edge movement and departure from the original pictures. Start with a bounded CPU test on frames 1–4; preserve high-frequency detail outside the residual field. Use a differentiable surrogate for reprojection error: the production verifier's tracker, masks and scorecard are not an end-to-end differentiable loss. Evaluate all 24 frames afterward using the unchanged verifier and compare unoptimized edges/appearance. This tests the geometry-cache principle behind GEN3C [G] without its large model; the main failure mode is an over-smoothed metric winner, which the image gate must reject.

**Generate material once, render the shot deterministically.** For the existing cube/sphere, project one generated reference onto their known UVs (or a small view-independent atlas), then render every frame using the original geometry, camera and lights. Begin with the non-emissive diffuse component; remove baked shading before treating colour as albedo, and leave unseen texels at their original material. Test whether object/camera matches and light direction survive while texture appearance changes; separately inspect seams, unseen surfaces and specular highlights. Only atlas creation is generative; repeated renders should reproduce identical bytes on the pinned renderer. This is the strongest route to literal camera/lighting locks, but a single projection cannot supply an unseen rainy street or correctly de-light arbitrary material. A one-object proof is enough for the first test.

## Tracking and reconstruction, swept 10/12

**2026-10-12 · Lane 3, T2 · Literature review, not a reproduced benchmark.** No weights downloaded and no GPU work. All external sources below were accessed **2026-10-12 PDT**; paper dates identify the publication month or conference. Tables preserve each paper's evaluation protocol. A learned metric-scale prior is not an independently measured survey. Monocular reconstruction without such a reference remains scale-ambiguous in general.

### Baseline and evidence boundary

The checked-out [sprint notes](END_TO_END_SPRINT.md#stage-7-real-time-camera-tracking-the-spike-1010), measured **2026-10-10**, report DPV-SLAM at **960 × 540: 1.8 cm RMS / 5.3 cm worst, 45 tracking FPS / 29 including final optimization, 0.77 GB allocated**; DROID-SLAM at that input size, internally resized, gives **1.3 / 2.3 cm, 335 tracking FPS / 30 including optimization, 3.2 GB**. These are a **24-frame synthetic dolly**, aligned to truth with a similarity, not a filmed-shot or absolute-scale test. DROID repeated bit-identically in that experiment; seeded DPVO varied by approximately **1 mm**. Retaining the chosen solve in the document is the existing repeatable-export contract.

The **T2 assignment dated 2026-10-12** supplies a later stage-9 reference: MASt3R-SLAM focal length **12–20% too long**, **11 FPS**, deterministic; survey check **1.35 px mean**; DPV-SLAM about **46 FPS** with known lens. These figures are **assignment-supplied, not independently verified here**: neither the worktree nor the read-only main copy of `END_TO_END_SPRINT.md` contained stage 9 at inspection. Their raw records and timing definitions must be recovered before the proposed experiment claims a comparison. Keep the earlier table above separate.

The merged [stage-7 L2 section](END_TO_END_SPRINT.md#stage-7-step-l2-the-lens-and-the-survey-1011) already implements shared focal/radial estimation with joint keyframe poses and points. Survey picks are tracked, triangulated, then used for similarity/scale **after** that refinement. Its measured matrix is still marked pending; the **<1% focal at 1920-wide** statement is a synthetic-track CPU test. [Brown–Conrady lens handling](3D_FOUNDATION.md#lens-distortion), Undistort/Redistort, [point tracking](ROTO_TRACKING.md#tracker), and existing [splat import/rendering](3D_ROADMAP.md#4-gaussian-splats) are useful integration foundations. ### 2. What can improve the current solve

**A sub-percent recovered focal length is an experiment target, not a property promised by any model above.** It is attainable on observable data: DroidCalib's TartanAir example [R20] demonstrates it. A near-straight dolly, planar scene, pure rotation, unknown crop, zoom or rolling shutter can leave focal/depth/distortion coupled. Fixing metric scale with one distance does not in itself remove that coupling. Measure focal in pixels after accounting for resize/crop; convert to millimetres only with the same known film back. Do not use a learned metric scale as proof of absolute position accuracy.

#### Lens: mechanism, incremental change and honest cost

1. **Finish the existing all-frame consistency experiment.** `nodebased/lensrefine.py` already minimizes robust reprojection over shared log-focal, radial terms, keyframe poses and points; `select_observations` caps the problem at **120 frames / 3,000 points**, and the three-stage Huber schedule is **24, 6, 1.5 px at 960-wide** (stage-7 L2, 2026-10-11). The current demo fits within the frame cap. Start by measuring this merged implementation against the exact saved stage-9 inputs; another network is not necessary to learn whether the bias is a missing refinement or an unobservable shot. Improve constraints through well-spread static tracks, longer feature lifetimes, coverage near image edges, and a shared fixed lens across the clip. Do not free principal point, tangential distortion and focal simultaneously on a weak shot. Cost: CPU optimization, track storage and dense reduced pose solves; wall time and host RAM unmeasured on real observations. Larger problems require sparse/block methods rather than simply removing caps. The CPU refinement is repeatable for fixed inputs, but it cannot repair DPVO's nondeterministic input tracks retroactively.

2. **Put the survey inside bundle adjustment.** Current survey handling fits scale/placement after triangulation. Add residuals for observed, fixed-world survey landmarks *during* the focal/pose fit, with weights from picking and measurement uncertainty. A surveyed distance alone constrains scale; independently known points distributed in image radius and depth provide stronger lens constraints. Keep unknown scene points free, treat surveyed locations as measured constraints rather than truth invented from the solve, and anchor the gauge consistently: surveyed world constraints replace the free similarity gauge, so do not also freeze an incompatible initial pose. Optimize shared focal first, then radial terms only if edge coverage supports them. Hold out independent observations/points from fitting and evaluate their reprojection; a lower training residual can coexist with a worse lens. Cost: a small residual/Jacobian extension to the existing CPU solver, operator coordinate conventions, and artist survey data. No new model/GPU required for replayed tracks. Position and focal targets remain unverified until measured; if the demo has too few independent landmarks, report insufficient evidence instead of tuning to its ground truth.

3. **A dedicated self-calibration pass followed by a fixed-intrinsics solve.** DroidCalib jointly refines intrinsics within DROID's measurement loop; this can correct matches that were biased by the initial lens, whereas postprocessing a frozen wrong-lens track set may not. Its published calibration results and memory limits are in R20; its full benchmarks are not all a safe fit on this card and AGPL integration needs separate consideration. COLMAP's shared-camera BA [R22] is a classical alternative for textured, static, parallax-rich footage; the R20 comparison gives measured cost, not a local runtime prediction. GeoCalib [R21] provides an independent initialization and uncertainty check when geometry is weak, but its several-degree FoV errors are not a sub-percent promise. Once a lens passes held-out checks, rerun the existing fast tracker with it fixed. Cost: a second solve, separate dependencies and calibration coordinate conversion; CPU replay cannot establish new frontend timing. For zooming footage, segment constant-lens intervals or explicitly fit a regularized time-varying focal model; a shared focal cannot describe the shot.

**Why a verifier feedback loop needs care.** The verifier's detector/matcher and robust fit are not an end-to-end differentiable loss. Reuse its pixel observations in a separate numerical objective, keep the production verifier unchanged, and score unseen observations afterward. Record observability through a focal profile (optimize nuisance variables at fixed focal values), rank/conditioning and stability under observation splits; a small formal covariance based on correlated tracks is an optimistic lower bound. A degenerate shot should return “lens unresolved” and retain the supplied lens, rather than report certainty from a low residual.

#### Dense geometry: a practical staged route

| Route | How it supplies the missing scene | Cost and failure mode |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Dense depth → fused proxy mesh**, first practical baseline | After the accepted camera/lens solve, use existing DROID depth or a small VGGT batch, reject moving/low-confidence/occluded pixels, align overlapping depth maps, and fuse in the surveyed coordinate frame. Export a mesh into `ReadGeo3D` for depth/normals/holdout conditioning. VGGT's camera+depth branch is the paper's preferred consistent geometry output [R5]. | Depth confidence is not calibrated uncertainty. Sparse DROID keyframes require more coverage; TSDF/surfel fusion and meshing need host memory and implementation work. Correct lens distortion before rays are fused. Thin structures, reflection, sky and unseen backs stay unknown. No runtime/quality win claimed. This supplies **observed background**, not geometry literally everywhere. |
| **Solved cameras → feed-forward splats**, quickest view-synthesis path | Feed calibrated, undistorted neighbouring views to MVSplat or NoPoSplat [R16,R17]; align/merge overlapping batches using the accepted cameras, then export supported Gaussian covariance/opacity/SH fields into `ReadSplat3D`. Their required calibration is now available. | Pairwise fast timing excludes whole-shot batching, fusion, export and overlap cleanup. Per-pair predictions may disagree in scale, opacity or appearance. Repeated Gaussians inflate density; naive concatenation produces ghosts. Memory and output size must be capped. Their static tests do not justify putting independently moving people into one splat map. |
| **Joint splat/camera refinement**, research follow-up | VGGT-GS SLAM [R19] initializes depth/poses then jointly optimizes splats, shared intrinsics/distortion and submap alignment. This directly addresses camera bias being absorbed by geometry. | Its default exceeds 12 GB; the small-submap measurements in the landscape show the accuracy/speed trade-off. Licence/release state remains unresolved here. Photometric fitting can absorb lighting and exposure changes into geometry or opacity. Keep it as a mechanism/reference, not a committed provider adoption. |
| **Dynamic scene background + time-indexed foreground** | MegaSaM, MonST3R or VGGT-Ω [R11,R13,R15] can produce time-varying geometry and camera estimates; separate a stable background proxy from foreground point/depth layers, with masks and time provenance. | Per-frame geometry is not a watertight animated mesh or consistent object identity. Nonrigid alignment/occlusion handling is additional work. MonST3R's cited configurations exceed budget, MegaSaM's local peak is unknown, and Ω's licences/size constrain use. Condition generation only on supported surfaces and expose coverage gaps. |

Existing splat relighting/shadow support does not turn baked RGB/SH into physical albedo. A reconstructed splat can be an occlusion proxy while still being a poor shadow receiver. Separate **visual colour**, **confidence-masked geometric depth**, and **material/normal estimates**; validate holdout boundaries and shadows against the original plate before using them to constrain generation. Capture quality and parallax set the ceiling; a plausible hallucinated surface must remain marked as inferred.

### 3. One worker-sized spike

[**Survey-constrained lens refinement against stage 9**](../briefs/proposed/T2-survey-constrained-lens.md) compares the saved provider solve, the already-merged L2 refinement, and one survey-in-the-fit variant. It has a **90-minute proposed budget**, fixed-input repeats, independent held-out survey points, explicit lens/position/speed/determinism gates and a known-lens fallback. The first gate is recovering the raw stage-9 artifacts; without them, a CPU synthetic replay is diagnostic only.
